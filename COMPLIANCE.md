# Regulatory Compliance Mapping

The system maps visual cues directly to the normative acts of the Republic of Kazakhstan. We do not hallucinate regulatory clauses.

## Rule 1: `cashier_away_from_workstation`
- **Visual Cue:** Cashier physically leaves the dynamic workstation zone for > 1.2s.
- **Presumed Violation:** Failure to secure the workplace, leaving cash desk unattended.
- **Normative Act:** Постановление Правления Национального Банка РК № 231 от 29.11.2019.
- **Article:** п. 22.
- **Clause Text:** *"Кассовому работнику не допускается покидать рабочее место до окончания обслуживания клиента."*
- **Adilet Link:** [https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680)

*(Note: We do not cite "leaning" or "phone use" because NBRK 231 does not have a direct, verifiable prohibition clause for these physical actions in this specific top-down context. We will not invent citations.)*
